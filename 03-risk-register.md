# Risk Register — Customer Churn Model

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sample too small (n=12) to generalize; model overfits or a lucky rule looks perfect by coincidence | High | High | Do not train/ship a production model on this file. Require a minimum sample size (e.g., hundreds of rows spanning several months) before retraining. | Data science lead |
| 2 | `plan_type` and `last_login_days` may leak the outcome (observed at/after the churn decision rather than in advance) | Medium–High | High | Confirm the timestamp of each feature relative to the churn label with the source system owner before reuse; exclude any feature that isn't knowable at the decision point. | Data engineering |
| 3 | Churn label definition (window, voluntary vs. involuntary) is undocumented | High | Medium | Get a written label definition from the business owner before further modeling; document it in the data card. | Product/business owner |
| 4 | `customer_id` is a direct identifier; risk of re-identification if data card or notebook is shared | Medium | Medium | Strip or hash the ID before any training, logging, sharing, or artifact creation. | Data science lead |
| 5 | `monthly_spend_inr` may act as a proxy for socioeconomic status, creating disparate impact if the model's outreach or offers vary by spend tier | Medium | Medium | Track outcomes (contact rate, offer value) by spend tier and plan tier once real data exists; review with a fairness lens before any pricing-adjacent action. | ML/fairness reviewer |
| 6 | Overtrust: 100% baseline accuracy on this file may cause stakeholders to skip real validation on production data | Medium | High | Present all metrics next to the toy-dataset caveat; require sign-off gate before any customer-facing action is automated. | Project sponsor |
| 7 | False negative (missed churner): customer leaves without being contacted | Depends on shipped threshold | High (lost recurring revenue) | Tune the decision threshold to favor recall for high-value customers; monitor actual churn vs. flagged-but-missed cases monthly. | Retention team + data science |
| 8 | False positive (unnecessary outreach/discount to a customer who wouldn't have churned) | Depends on shipped threshold | Low–Medium (wasted discount/contact cost) | Cap the false-positive rate and track discount cost per retained customer to keep the program net-positive. | Retention team |
| 9 | No consent/provenance documentation for this data's use in modeling | Unknown | High (compliance) | Confirm with data owner that the terms of service / privacy notice cover this analytical use before any real customer data is processed this way. | Legal/compliance |
| 10 | Model or rule used to justify punitive action (e.g., account restriction) beyond its intended scope | Low (if safeguards followed) | High | Explicitly restrict allowed actions in the deployment policy to "flag for human-led retention outreach" only. | Project sponsor |
